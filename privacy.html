Life After Dashboards – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This page explains how we use the details you share when you register for a Life After Dashboards live event on LinkedIn: only to run the event and keep you informed about it, and never to sell your data.

Who we are

Life After Dashboards is a live show hosted by Dânia Meira and José Coto. We are the organizers of the event and are responsible for the registration data we receive from LinkedIn. Contact: meira.dania@gmail.com.

What we receive

When you register for an event, we collect only the fields below. You provide them yourself; we do not collect your LinkedIn password or private messages.

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Company name
  • Business email
  • Job title
  • Industry
  • Country/Region

How we use it

  • To run the event: count expected attendees and plan the session.
  • To contact you about the event: reminders, the link to join, and the recording after the show.
  • To shape the show: understand which topics, job titles, industries and regions our audience represents, using the event's LinkedIn analytics and aggregated figures rather than individual profiles.
  • To invite you to future episodes, only if you tell us you want that.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not share individual registrations with sponsors or other third parties.
  • We do not use your data for automated decisions or profiling.
  • We do not copy registrations into marketing lists without asking you first.

Legal basis

We process registration data to run an event you chose to sign up for (our legitimate interest in organizing it, and yours in attending). We send anything beyond event logistics only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it is stored and for how long

Registration details stay in LinkedIn and we read them through the analytics of the event itself on LinkedIn. We do not keep a separate database of registrants. Any export or note we make from them is deleted within 90 days after the event, unless you asked to hear about future episodes. LinkedIn handles your data under its own Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, to stop contacting you, or to restrict how we use it. Write to us at the contact above and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use registration data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change applies.
